# STARTER — 2028 Ford Falcon AU Aeroblade

**SPECULATIVE:** evolve AU New Edge design into a 2028 Australian sedan with Colani-inspired aerodynamic thinking. The governing direction is **sharper**; this is a new-generation concept.

## Current decisions

- **DECISION:** crisp blade nose, inverted/scalene triangular headlamps with the long side outboard, and TS50-inspired front aggression.
- **DECISION:** integrated AU-style rear wing, lamps reaching towards the edges and the accepted shoulder/taillamp relationship.
- **DECISION:** retain the intended door-handle identity; modern seals and mirrors; latest side-glass direction has **no extra rear-quarter window**.
- **DECISION:** interior retains an AU-derived oval centre and manual handbrake; no rotary selector, electronic parking brake or pale wood.
- **DECISION:** range ideas are Forte, S, Futura, Fairmont Ghia (Amaretto) and XR8. They remain variants within this brief.
