# STARTER — 1989 compact Jaguar

**SPECULATIVE:** a compact Jaguar saloon imagined for a 1989 launch, positioned against period compact executive cars. “Original X-Type” is a creative shorthand, not a documented Jaguar programme name.

## Current decisions

- **DECISION:** Concept C targets approximately **4,300 mm long, 1,690 mm wide, 1,340 mm high; 2,560 mm wheelbase**. These are design targets, not production specifications.
- **DECISION:** deep aubergine; squared quad-headlamp surrounds; recessed rectangular XJ40-inspired handles; narrow roof/tumblehome.
- **DECISION:** rounded extra rear-quarter window without a Hofmeister kink; real flying buttresses; short two-and-a-half-box bobtail with a real boot.
- **DECISION:** shortened smoked XJ40-inspired rear lamps and 15-inch smooth-boss turbine wheels.
- **DECISION:** preserve the compact body through the imagined 1994–95 facelift; resolve exterior geometry before interior work.
