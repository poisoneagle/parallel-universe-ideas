# Research and evidence

This repository currently preserves creative briefs and research leads. **No historical claim has been promoted to DOCUMENTED in this first pass.** “Needs sources” means evidence has not been attached here; it does not imply no earlier research exists.

Put each claim register in its canonical concept or editorial entry. Create a separate research dossier only when the evidence becomes too substantial for that file.

## Claim register format

| Claim ID | Exact claim | Label | Source / locator | What it establishes / limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| example-01 | One narrowly worded assertion | UNVERIFIED | Not yet attached | Lead only; exclude from factual narration |

For a **DOCUMENTED** claim, record source title, author/organisation, publication date where known, URL or repository file link, page/timestamp/section, and date checked. Prefer original manufacturer material, contemporary records and attributable interviews. Treat forum/social testimony as testimony; multiple copies of one story are not independent corroboration.

Keep the supported wording narrow. Evidence that a vehicle concept existed does not establish production approval, a particular platform, factory allocation or commercial viability. Label conflicting sources and reconstructions explicitly.

## Initial research boundaries

- Bangkok Commodores: distinguish identifiable vehicles and attributable testimony from “Holden Hoon Team” folklore. Allegations remain unverified.
- Holden alternatives: establish which Torana, Crewman, One Tonner, Nullarbor and HSV diesel claims were real proposals, and which are our reconstructions.
- Concept briefs: check donor dimensions, model-year changes and engine identities before using them as factual context. Imagined specifications remain speculative.

## Sources, assets and publication

Record each reference asset's original URL/location, creator, version and usage permission. A filename recovered from chat is not a usable attachment or permission to republish. Keep master renders untouched; record new versions and approval separately.

Release requires factual assertions to be sourced or explicitly removed/qualified, speculative content to be labelled, and included media to be checked. Record the published URL and date in the editorial entry once actually published.
